Audit and sources

All statistics come from the nflverse weekly player box-score feed. Rosters come from nflverse weekly rosters; injury designations come from nflverse injury reports. These are community-maintained data releases and are not an independent verification against every official NFL box score. Player records are joined using their GSIS ID, not by matching names. One row per player; traded players' season statistics include their former and current teams.

GP* = number of weeks in which a player's statistical row is available. This is not guaranteed to equal official games played (especially offensive linemen and special teams). An empty statistic means unreported/unsupported, not zero. Statistics may include games from a partially completed current week. Practice participation does not automatically mean an official doubtful or out designation.
